# Rock face of an excavation

Date: 18 November 2026. Written in the working tree of `feat/core-pick-coarsening`, not committed, after the changes of 17 November in the same package.

## Change impact

**Request.** The user built a rock wall generator outside this repository (SpaceXR V25 to V26.2, a Babylon Playground script for a 4 km cylindrical pit) and asked to apply that rendering to the visible, inner face of the excavation built here the day before (`growChamber`), through a builder that takes any mesh and transforms it. The user left the name open; it is `buildRockFace`.

**Components.** A new module of `@spacexr/ceiling`, `rock-face.ts`, exported from the package entry. Nothing existing changes behaviour: `growChamber` and `growSurface` are not modified, and a caller that does not call `buildRockFace` gets the same meshes as before. Still no dependency, no renderer, CPU only, once per excavation. `SPX-DEM-001` is not touched. The federated component index (`../atmosphere-core-r1-7-1/docs/architecture/COMPONENT_INDEX.md`) is not present in this workspace and could not be read.

**Execution path (new).**

1. The mesh is refined by midpoint subdivision, every triangle in four, until no edge is longer than the edge length asked for. The topology and the winding are kept.
2. The normal of each vertex is the area-weighted sum of the normals of its faces, in the winding of the mesh. `towardsRock` says whether that normal points into the rock (the default, as `growChamber` winds its surface outwards) or into the void.
3. Each vertex moves along that normal into the rock by the recession of the rock field at its displaced position, found by three fixed-point steps as in V25, then softly limited to `maxDisplacement` (a hyperbolic tangent, no flat plateau). With `within`, an `IVolume`, a move that would leave the volume is shortened by bisection: the Studio gives the volume under `ground - minDepth`, so that the rock does not bring a chamber nearer the ground than its smallest depth.
4. Normals are computed again on the displaced mesh.

**Rock field.** A port of the `geology` field of V25 (rotated, domain-warped 3D value noise with ridges, two scales of jittered Voronoi fissures, bedding ledges on warped horizons offset by four global fault planes, recession in the damage zone of each fault). It is evaluated in the frame of the mesh with a `scale`: `field_s(p) = s * field(p / s)`, so the pit of V25 is `scale = 1` and a smaller excavation keeps the same look at a smaller size. Octaves and fissures whose wavelength is under `minWavelength` are faded out, because a mesh of a given edge length cannot carry them: a renderer adds the detail under that wavelength, per pixel, and `buildRockFace` returns that wavelength for it.

**Invariants.** Lengths are metres in the frame of the mesh, which the caller gives with its `up` axis. The same mesh and options give the same result. The output has the same topology as the refined mesh and the winding of the input. Every vertex moves along its own normal by at most `maxDisplacement`.

**Known limits, not handled.** No test against self-intersection: a `maxDisplacement` larger than the local thickness of the void, or than the radius of curvature of the surface, can fold it. The caller keeps `maxDisplacement` under the clearance of its content. Faults and horizons are those of V25, fixed in the field, not read from a geological model.

**Costs.** No measurement: `COST_NOT_ISOLATED`. The field costs about 30 noise evaluations and two Voronoi searches of 27 cells per call, four calls per vertex. Each refinement pass multiplies the faces by four.

**Rationale.** The split between geometry carried by the mesh and detail added per pixel comes from V26.2, where the same look was kept with 15 times fewer triangles. The fade over one octave below `minWavelength` and the three fixed-point steps are taken from those scripts: `RATIONALE_UNKNOWN` beyond their visual result. The constants of the field (wavelengths, amplitudes, fault planes, seeds) are those of V25 and are `RATIONALE_UNKNOWN`: they were tuned by eye by the user.

## Verification

`rock-face.test.ts`: refinement to the edge length on a closed mesh (topology of a sphere kept), the displacement bounded by `maxDisplacement` and along the normal into the rock, `towardsRock` reversed, the `up` axis, the volume `within`, repeatability, scale invariance of the field, the fade under `minWavelength`, and a chamber of `growChamber` dressed. 76 tests of the package pass, with typecheck, lint and build. Nothing was rendered in an engine in this package; the rendering is in the Studio of geomodels-explorer (`src/babylon/underground/rock-face-material.ts`), where a chamber of 1000 m dressed this way has 36 160 triangles and takes about 0.25 s to build in a browser, one measurement on one machine.
